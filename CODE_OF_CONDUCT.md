# Code of conduct / 行为准则

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project. Be constructive, discuss technical
decisions rather than people, respect different experience levels and languages, and never post
someone else's private data or restricted assets.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized content,
deliberate disruption, credential disclosure, and redistribution of models or media without
permission. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and may restrict participation when
necessary to protect the community.

本项目致力于建立尊重他人、无骚扰的协作环境。请建设性沟通，讨论技术而非攻击个人，尊重
不同经验水平和语言背景，不得发布他人隐私或受限素材。

不可接受的行为包括骚扰、威胁、歧视性语言、性化内容、蓄意扰乱、泄露凭据，以及未经许可
再分发模型或媒体。为保护社区，维护者可编辑、隐藏或删除内容，并在必要时限制参与。
